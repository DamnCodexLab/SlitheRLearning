Slitherbot VH, 17 modules

New rib with the square 4-hole leg and the new coupler. V modules (servo axis vertical) swing side to side on wheels; H modules (coupler rolled 90°, oval rib with a wire collector underneath, no wheels) pitch up and down to lift the head.

Raise the head (H joints)

An MG90S stalls at about 1.8 kgf·cm (4.8 V). Lifting from the last H joint needs about half of that; lifting from further back overloads the servo. Watch the load column below.

Angles are limited to ±55°, where the new rib starts to hit its own coupler and servo. Load is the gravity torque the servo must hold, as a percentage of the 1.8 kgf·cm stall torque; above about 50% the servo runs hot.
